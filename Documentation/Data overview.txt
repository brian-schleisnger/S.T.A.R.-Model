Current data sources
- Acquisition file: this has per-customer data going back to 4Q 2018, for most of the unique activations on Dish TV. it contains the customer's attributed information (account id, city/state/zipcode, geobucket, credit score range, package, activation plan, sales channel, etc.), the subscriber acquisition cost attributed to that customer (marketing spend, activation incentives, installation, etc.), as well as (not my but a whole separate team's) model projected future data (estimated churn, arpu, costs, monthly cash flow, and npv value), all pulled at the end of the month they activated so the cash flow type stuff is all estimates. This data is managed by Kyle Morrison's Team
- Marketing spend file: this has by-month spend for the marketing (and some from sales) department, broken out by account(digital, mail, etc.) and activity (for digital: search, social, etc.). This data is managed by Tom Keller's team
- Subscriber count file: this is a month by month counts of various subscriber counts: total subscribers at beginning and end of month, average subscribers, monthly churn rates, deactivations, and activations broken out by all channels. This data was pulled directly from OneStream
- Sales file: This is a day-by-day count of sales calls, sales, direct adds, and buyer's remorse. This data is managed by Trevor Karne's Team
- Dish P&L file: this contains month by month income statement data, such as revenues, expenses, SG&A, and operating incomes. All data was pulled from OneStream base on the template from Logan Crum's team Executive forecast.
